About Us

HelpsAap IT Services is a technology services business based in Kochi, Kerala, India. We build mobile apps, websites and software for people and small businesses, and we also provide IT support.

Our mission

To build simple, reliable digital tools that make everyday needs easier to meet.

Our vision

Useful technology that is easy to use, respects people's privacy and is available in the languages they speak, including English and Malayalam.

Our technology

Flutter and Dart for mobile and web apps, cloud databases for secure data storage, and standard web technologies for websites.
